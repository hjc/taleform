issue src/builders,test-vault/Missions material-bug dm loud parity baseline for the Declarative Model wayfinding medium medium medium The Adventure Map builder gives two groups of cards identical coordinates, so one card hides the other: every beat with two or more outcomes stacks its IF cards, and a long-jump branch label stacks on the beat's award or combat XP card. It has been true since the builder was written. It fails visibly, as a map where one card's text cannot be read until the DM drags the top card away, and no build or check reports it.

Card positions are computed per card with no check against cards already placed. Three placements share coordinates:

  • Every IF card of a beat gets (2*SIDE, y(beat)+50); only the node id (if|beat|k) varies with k.
  • A long-jump label gets (LX, y(beat)+60), the same slot as the beat's award card.
  • A beat with no award puts the combat card at (LX, y(beat)+60) too, since y advances by 130 only after an award card.
